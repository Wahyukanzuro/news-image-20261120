One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: 35 stories
across six continents — Cyclone Akvan's landfall in Odisha, Russia's barrage
on Kyiv's bridges, COP31's final day without a deal, and Asia's AI sell-off —
plus a world map, a by-the-numbers strip, and a date check that keeps
Thursday's news out.

`main.ts` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, drawn in HTML/CSS
and rendered with Playwright) to the output directory. No network, no
randomness — both runs write identical bytes.
